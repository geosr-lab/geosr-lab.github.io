Geotechnical Soil Report

GeoSR

Lab-sheet style tools for Soil Mechanics practicals and geotechnical calculations, with every step, chart, and source shown.

The tools themselves are in Indonesian and follow the Soil Mechanics lab forms at Universitas Jambi. Numbers use a decimal comma (12,5 means 12.5).

6 calculators

Axial capacity of bored piles from SPT

Reese & Wright (1977) method with a soil profile and step-by-step calculation per layer.

Lateral capacity of piles (Broms method)

Broms (1964) method: short, intermediate, or long pile failure mode, with a Pult–length chart.

Bearing capacity of shallow foundations

General bearing capacity equation with shape and depth factors and a groundwater correction.

Consolidation settlement

Normally and over-consolidated clay, e–log σ' diagram, and settlement over time.

Soil parameters from SPT N-values

N60 and (N1)60 corrections, then Dr, φ, E, cu, and vs from empirical correlations.

Soil parameters from CPT (sondir)

From qc and fs: Dr and φ for sand; cu, St, OCR, CR, vs for clay.
