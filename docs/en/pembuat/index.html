About me

Defsa Yurinda, undergraduate Civil Engineering student at the Faculty of Science and Technology, Universitas Jambi, Indonesia (7th semester in 2026). My main interest is geotechnical engineering, especially pile foundations and soil testing. I built GeoSR so that Soil Mechanics lab data and geotechnical practice calculations can be worked out with clear steps and sources anyone can check.

  • Now: Laboratory Assistant for the Soil Mechanics practical, odd semester 2026/2027.
  • Award: 2nd place, HATTI 2026 Student Geotechnical Innovation Competition.
  • Final project: axial capacity of bored piles from SPT data compared with static load tests.

Project

GeoSR (this site). Eighteen tools for Soil Mechanics lab data, six geotechnical calculators, and a bank of practice problems. Every formula names its source, and the lab tools are tested automatically against the numbers in sample lab reports.
